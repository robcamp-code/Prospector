**Business Name:** FixHub Tech Repair
**Description:** A phone, tablet, and laptop repair kiosk chain offering same-day screen replacements, battery swaps, and data recovery at student-friendly prices.
**Target Customers:** College students, young adults, and gig workers aged 18-35 who rely on their devices and want cheap, fast repairs.
**Pricing Tier:** Budget.
**Monthly Lease Budget:** $1,500–$3,000 per kiosk.
**Preferred Location:** Nationwide — open to any market with large college-student populations, dense young renter neighborhoods, and mall or campus-adjacent foot traffic.
