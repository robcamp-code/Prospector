**Business Name:** Spinback Records & Coffee
**Description:** A retail and cafe hybrid selling new and used vinyl records, turntables, and specialty coffee, hosting weekly listening parties and local artist showcases.
**Target Customers:** Music enthusiasts aged 20-45, creatives, students, and remote workers who linger over coffee.
**Pricing Tier:** Mid-range.
**Monthly Lease Budget:** $4,000–$6,000.
**Preferred Location:** Trendy, walkable arts district in Portland, Oregon, near live music venues, bars, and independent shops with heavy foot traffic.
