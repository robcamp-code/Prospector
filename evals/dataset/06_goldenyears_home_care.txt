**Business Name:** Golden Years Home Care
**Description:** A senior home-healthcare agency providing in-home nursing visits, medication management, companionship, and mobility assistance for aging adults who want to stay in their homes.
**Target Customers:** Seniors aged 70+ and their adult children who arrange and pay for care; middle-to-upper-income retiree households.
**Pricing Tier:** Mid-range to premium.
**Monthly Lease Budget:** $2,500–$4,000 (small administrative office).
**Preferred Location:** Anywhere in Florida with dense retiree populations, high concentrations of seniors living independently, and nearby hospitals.
